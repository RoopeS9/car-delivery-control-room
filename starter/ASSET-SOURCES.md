# Vehicle illustrations and make marks

The 31 vehicle images in `public/car-images/` were generated for this fictional challenge. Every model in the bundled dataset has an image. Each image represents a model, not the specific vehicle, its colour, condition, location or handover evidence. The record page labels them accordingly. Orders with the same make and model share an illustration.

The make marks in `public/car-logos/` identify real vehicle manufacturers in the sample data. They do not imply that the manufacturers sponsor or endorse the challenge. Fifteen marks were adapted to a single ink colour from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) revision `c71c69e3a27e4436936b831c72643e6513b9f49b` (CC0 1.0). The Mercedes-Benz star came from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mercedes-Benz_free_logo.svg). The marks remain trademarks of their owners.
