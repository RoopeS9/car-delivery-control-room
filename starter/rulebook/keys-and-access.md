# Keys and access

## When it applies

A driver asks how to get in at a pickup or delivery place, where to leave or
collect keys, who to hand over to, or reports that a gate, desk or contact
is not where the records say.

## Facts to collect, in order

1. **Which end**: pickup or delivery, and which order.
2. **The recorded instruction** for that end (`get_order_context`): access
   instruction, contact name and role, opening hours, and **how confirmed**
   it is. A site profile's standard instruction is not a confirmation for
   this car. Say which it is.
3. **What the driver sees** on site, in their words.

## What you may do alone

- Answer from the records when the instruction is recorded and its source is
  clear. Quote it and say where it comes from.
- Ask the site (`ask_site`) when it is not the customer.
- Close the case when the driver has what they need.

## When to stop and hand over

- The record says the contact or access is unconfirmed and the driver is
  already there or about to travel.
- What the driver sees disagrees with the record.
- The site is the customer and you would need to ask them something.
- Anyone asks the driver to hand the car or keys to a different person than
  the record names.

## Never

Never tell a driver to leave keys anywhere the record does not say. Never
accept a change of receiver from someone other than the customer.

## Worked example

Driver at the delivery: "Where do I leave the keys?" Record: "Reception must
receive the keys and sign the vehicle handover", from the site profile, not
confirmed for this order. Answer: "The site's standard instruction is to
hand the keys to reception and have them sign the handover. That is from
their profile, not confirmed for this car, so please get the signature
before you leave." Close the case if the driver confirms.
