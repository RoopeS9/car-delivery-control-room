# Accident

## When it applies

Any collision, however small: another vehicle, a barrier, an animal, a
parking knock, or damage the driver found after leaving the car.

## Facts to collect, in order

1. **People first.** Is anyone hurt? If anyone may be hurt, the only message
   is the safety instruction: "Call 112 now. Stay out of traffic." Nothing
   else until help is on the way.
2. **Is the driver safe** and out of traffic?
3. **Police**: have they been called or are they needed (injury, blocked
   road, the other party leaves, disagreement)?
4. **The other party**: name, plate and insurer as the driver reads them,
   if there is one. A photo of documents is better than typing.
5. **Photos**: all sides of our car, the other vehicle, the scene.
6. **Can the car be driven?** The driver's description only. Do not decide
   it yourself.
7. **The order**: owner, promise, later bookings.

## What you may do alone

Safety instructions (including calling 112), questions and photo requests,
lookups.

## When to stop and hand over

Immediately if anyone is hurt. Otherwise once the scene is safe and the
photos and other-party details are in.

## Who decides

The operator decides next steps and talks to the owner (`CONTACT_CUSTOMER`).
Whether the car may be driven on is an operator decision
(`CLEAR_TO_CONTINUE`). Insurance and costs are for the operator.

## Never

Never say who was at fault. Never tell the driver to sign anything at the
scene beyond the standard accident report. Never tell the driver the car is
fine to drive.
