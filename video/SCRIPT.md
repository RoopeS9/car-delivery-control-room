# Video script, version 2

Replaces section 1 of `SUBMISSION-DRAFTS.md`. Target **2:50, hard limit 3:00** (the brief says two to three minutes).
Everything is spoken in English, with English subtitles burned in. The one exception is the stop line on Leo’s phone, which
the app now sends in the driver’s language (Leo lives in Tampere in the data), so it should arrive in Finnish with English
underneath. The other stream was still confirming that with a real model, so **check it on a real run before you film it.**

**The idea.** Open with the numbers and a confident line. Then a short comedy: an operator with their feet up while the
simulator, a gremlin with no shame, throws problems at the desk and the agent quietly handles them. One driver panics;
the agent calms the driver down. One card needs the operator, who sits forward for the first time. Then the control room
and a timelapse of problems appearing and clearing on the map. We end on camera: process, limits, next steps, invitation.

## Filming with a phone

- **Landscape, 4K at 30 fps, always.** Lock exposure and focus (hold the screen). Wipe the lens.
- **Sound is most of it.** Record dialogue close to the mouth (a wired or wireless lav mic, or the phone a hand’s width away,
  never across a room). Record 20 seconds of silence in each place (room tone). Clap once at the start of each take.
- **Car scene:** stay parked, handbrake on, hazards on, daylight, no moving shots from inside. A car park or a quiet lay-by.
  Do not film in a service station or on private property without asking. Hide real number plates and keep strangers out
  of the frame, because the video is public.
- **Prop for the dashboard:** a second phone propped behind the wheel showing `starter/public/sim/dashboard-misfire.jpg`.
- **Every actor’s line twice:** once for the camera, once for a clean audio take.
- Shoot **cutaways** (hands, mug, screen over the shoulder, feet on the desk) for 5–8 seconds each. They are what saves the edit.

## Final Cut

1. Project: 1920×1080, 30 fps, stereo. Import screen recordings at their own size and scale to fit.
2. Order of work: assemble the dialogue, then the screen inserts, then the music, then the sound effects, last the captions.
3. Music sits 18–22 dB below dialogue and ducks under every line. The cue changes land on the cuts (see `MUSIC.md`).
4. Burn in English subtitles: judges may watch with the sound off. Use the app’s fonts (Unbounded for captions, Poppins for text)
   and colours (cream `#fffef7`, ink `#12110e`, orange `#b04617`) so the cards look like part of the product.
5. Export H.264, 1080p, about 16 LUFS. Check the total is **under 3:00 including the end card.**
6. Watch it once muted and once with the phone speaker.

## Things not to show or say

- A mocked-up app screen. Every insert is the real app, from a real run.
- Savings as fact. Say “on our estimates” and keep the footnote.
- Real people, plates or brands in the background; any key or secret.
- That the agent “fixed the car” or told the driver it is safe to drive on. It does neither.
- The timelapse as a measurement. Keep its caption.
